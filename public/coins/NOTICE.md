# Coin artwork provenance

EdFi displays these local SVGs only to identify the corresponding assets in market and demo UI. They are curated artwork from the projects listed below, not a claim that a coin issuer supplied or approved them.

## Identity and trademark scope

The BNB asset is the BNB Chain token mark, not the Binance exchange logo or corporate wordmark. It is used beside the BNB ticker, never as EdFi branding. [BNB Chain's brand guidelines](https://www.bnbchain.org/en/brand-guidelines) describe token/icon use and restrictions against implying endorsement, and ask projects to seek approval for logo use. No issuer approval or broader trademark permission is asserted here.

The collections' licenses cover their licensed artwork; they do not establish sponsorship, affiliation, issuer approval, or ownership of third-party trademarks. CC0 explicitly excludes trademark rights. Asset names and marks remain associated with their respective projects. This notice records source and intended use, not legal clearance.

EDC retains EdFi's existing mortarboard in `src/components/Logo.jsx`. The navigation icons and neutral unknown-asset fallback are original EdFi UI artwork and are not part of this third-party collection.
